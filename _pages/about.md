I am Ayse Tursucular, a 3rd-year PhD student in [Machine Learning](https://ml.gatech.edu/) in the [H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering](https://www.isye.gatech.edu/) at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://www.gatech.edu/), advised by [Prof. Nagi Gebraeel](https://sites.gatech.edu/nagi/). Previously, I completed a B.Sc. in Industrial Engineering and a B.A. in Economics at [Koç University](https://www.ku.edu.tr/en/).

My research focuses on causal inference and federated learning in interconnected physical systems. I am particularly interested in:

<div style="margin-left: 1.5em;" markdown="1">
**(i) Distributed & Federated Learning:** how models can be learned collaboratively across heterogeneous physical systems.

**(ii) Causal Discovery & Inference:** theory and methods for learning causal relationships and estimating causal effects.

**(iii) Dynamic Network Inference:** how interdependencies among interconnected dynamical systems can be identified and interpreted from data.
</div>
